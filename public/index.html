How it works

Press Mog Now. We queue you for a real opponent — a real human, never a bot. If nobody's around, you wait. Once matched, a three-count runs, then you each get one turn to speak — never at the same time, so neither voice contaminates the other's reading. VoiceMog measures your real fundamental frequency (F0) — the lowest, most honest number your voice gives up. Lower Hz wins. Ranked matches move your Elo; casual doesn't. Win streaks unlock titles. Lose, and you run it back.

Your voice is processed live in your browser to calculate pitch. Nothing is recorded or stored — the audio is discarded the instant the number is measured.
